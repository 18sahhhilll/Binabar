How a round works

01

Listen

A 10 to 15 second instrumental clip plays. No vocals, just the beat. You can replay it up to 3 times.

02

Guess

Type your answer. Autocomplete helps you pick. You get 3 attempts before the answer is revealed.

03

Score

100 points for a clean guess, minus 25 for each hint you use. After every round, you see the full credits.

Scoring and hints

100 Points, no hints
75 1 hint used
50 2 hints used
25 3 hints used

Three hints per round

  • Artist initial: the first letter of the main artist's name
  • Release year: the year the track came out
  • Language: the primary language of the track

Each hint costs 25 points. Wrong answer or skip scores 0.
