How it works

A quick rundown of how Binabar plays.

The game

Each game is 10 rounds. In each round, you hear a short instrumental clip (10 to 15 seconds, beats only, no vocals) from an Indian hip hop track.

Your job: name the track.

Guessing

Type your answer in the text field. Autocomplete will help you find the right track. Matching is case-insensitive, ignores punctuation and extra spaces.

You get 3 attempts per round. After 3 wrong guesses, the answer is revealed.

You can also skip a round if you are stuck, but you score 0.

Audio

The clip plays when you press the play button. You can replay it up to 3 times per round. Audio never plays automatically.

Hints

Each round gives you access to 3 hints:

  • Artist initial: the first letter of the main artist's name
  • Release year: the year the track came out
  • Language: the primary language of the track

Each hint costs 25 points from your round score.

Scoring

Scenario Points
Correct, no hints100
Correct, 1 hint75
Correct, 2 hints50
Correct, 3 hints25
Wrong answer or skip0

Credits

After every round, you see the full credits: title, artist, producer, label, year, and a link to listen to the original track. Every track in the game is also listed on the Credits page.

Best score

Your best score is saved in your browser (localStorage). No accounts needed.
